This past weekend, Audacy earned six honors from the 2026 South Carolina Broadcasters Association STAR Awards. The STAR Awards celebrate excellence in South Carolina broadcasting and highlight stations that inform, entertain, and inspire the local community. The Award Show was held on Saturday, August 1st, at the Medallion Conference Center in Columbia, South Carolina.
