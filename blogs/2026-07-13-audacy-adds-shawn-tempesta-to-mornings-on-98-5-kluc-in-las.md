LAS VEGAS, NV – July 13, 2026 – 98.5 KLUC (KLUC-FM), an Audacy station in Las Vegas, has named Shawn Tempesta as its new morning show host. He will be on-air weekdays from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m.
