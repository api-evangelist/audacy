Blake Whiten has been named Audacy’s newest Country LAUNCH artist, joining Zach John King, Stephen Wilson Jr. and Audrey Hobert as part of the 2026 LAUNCH artist class. Audacy’s music discovery program LAUNCH leverages its multiplatform reach and the power of radio to introduce new artists to its millions of listeners and followers.
