BALTIMORE, MD – July 24, 2026 – 105.7 The Fan (WJZ-FM), an Audacy station in Baltimore, launches “The Final Drive,” a new afternoon show with Femi Ayanbadejo. Ayanbadejo will co-host the show with Ken Weinman and Tim Barbalace weekdays from 2:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
