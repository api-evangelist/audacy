PITTSBURGH, PA – September 8, 2026 – WAMO 107.3 (WAMO-FM), an Audacy station in Pittsburgh, elevates KiKi B. Jones to Brand Manager, where she will lead the station’s content strategy, talent, operations and branding. Jones will continue to host the station’s midday show weekdays from 10:00 a.m.
