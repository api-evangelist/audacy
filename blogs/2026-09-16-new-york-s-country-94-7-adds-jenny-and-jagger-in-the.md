New York’s Country 94.7 (WXBK-HD2), an Audacy station in New York, adds “Jenny and Jagger in the Morning,” featuring Jenny Matthews, Jagger and Matt Gapske to its weekday lineup. The trio can be heard weekday mornings from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m.
